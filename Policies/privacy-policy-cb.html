ChemScale Privacy Policy

App Name: ChemScale  ·  Bundle ID: com.chembalance.app

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

Thank you for choosing ChemScale ("the App"), a chemical equation balancing trainer. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

In short: ChemScale is a fully offline educational app. It collects, stores, and transmits no personal data. There is no account system, no tracking, no advertising, and no analytics.

1. Information We Collect

ChemScale does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App is fully offline and does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or location.

No data is gathered from your device, no usage is recorded on any server, and no information is shared with or sold to any third party — because there is no data to share.

2. Local Data Only

All data used by the App is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. This includes:

  • Your practice statistics (questions answered and correct counts per difficulty level)
  • Your wrong-answer book and review history
  • Your daily challenge progress and streak
  • Your unlocked achievements and best timed-mode records
  • App preferences

This information is stored using your device's standard local storage (e.g., UserDefaults) and is completely removed when you delete the App or clear the App's data.

3. No Third-Party Services

ChemScale does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not use cookies, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies. The App does not connect to any server, so no data can be collected or processed by external parties.

4. Network & Permissions

The App makes no network requests and requests no sensitive device permissions (such as camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location).

5. Children's Privacy

ChemScale is an educational tool suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because the App collects no personal information and performs no online communication, it is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. No parental consent is required to use the App.

6. Data Security

Because all information remains on your device, it is protected by the security features of your operating system. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and installing the latest version of the App.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Delete data: Delete your in-App progress at any time from the App's Stats screen, or remove all data by uninstalling the App.
  • Access data: Since no data is stored by us, there is no data for us to access or provide.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

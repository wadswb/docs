PRIVACY POLICY

ChemBalance Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

Thank you for using ChemBalance ("the App"). Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ChemBalance handles information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

ChemBalance does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App is fully offline and does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Local Data Only

All data used by the App is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. This includes:

  • Your practice statistics (questions answered and correct counts per difficulty level)
  • Your wrong-answer book and review history
  • Your daily challenge progress and streak
  • Your unlocked achievements and best timed-mode records
  • App preferences

This information is stored using your device's standard local storage (e.g., UserDefaults) and can be completely removed by deleting the App or clearing the App's data.

3. No Third-Party Services

ChemBalance does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not use cookies, web beacons, or similar tracking technologies. Since no data is sent to any server, there is nothing to share with third parties.

4. Network & Permissions

The App does not make any network requests and does not request any sensitive device permissions (such as camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location).

5. Children's Privacy

ChemBalance is an educational tool suitable for users of all ages, including children. Because the App collects no personal information and performs no online communication, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. No parental consent is required to use the App.

6. Data Security

Because all information remains on your device, it is protected by the security features of your operating system. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Delete data: You may delete your in-App progress at any time from the App's Stats / Settings screen, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Access data: Since no data is stored by us, there is no data for us to access or provide.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

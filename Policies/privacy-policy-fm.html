Privacy Policy

FabricMate values your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is stored, and the choices you have.

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

FabricMate ("we", "our", or "the app") is a fabric usage estimation tool designed for apparel cutting, pattern making, and layout planning. Your trust is important to us, and we have built the app with a privacy-first approach: FabricMate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

In short: this app works entirely on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no data leaves your iPhone or iPad.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically, the app:

  • Does not require an account or registration.
  • Does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • Does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers.
  • Does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

Because the app has no network access, none of your usage data is ever transmitted over the internet.

3. Data Storage

All data you create — such as order items, calculation results, and history entries — is stored locally on your device only, using standard iOS on-device storage (UserDefaults).

  • Your data stays on your device and is never uploaded to any server.
  • History is capped at the most recent 50 entries to keep the app lightweight.
  • You can delete individual history entries at any time, or clear the entire history from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data will permanently remove all locally stored information.

4. Third-Party Services

FabricMate does not integrate with any third-party services, frameworks, or SDKs. There is nothing to disclose regarding data shared with third parties, because no data is collected in the first place.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The app contains no advertisements and performs no analytics or tracking. We do not use device identifiers (such as IDFA) for any purpose, and we do not employ cookies or similar technologies.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. As no data is collected, there is no special handling required and no parental consent is needed.

7. Security

Because all information is stored on your device and never transmitted, the risk of unauthorized access or data interception is minimized. The security of your on-device data is protected by the built-in security features of your iOS device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's functionality or applicable law. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Significant changes will be communicated through an in-app notice or an updated policy description.

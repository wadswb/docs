ConcreteWiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026 · Effective as of the date above

Overview

No Account Required On-Device Only No Data Sold

ConcreteWiz (also referred to as "the App") is an on-site concrete mix design calculator built for construction professionals and self-builders. ConcreteWiz performs all calculations directly on your device and does not operate a backend server, a user authentication system, or an analytics pipeline.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ConcreteWiz processes, where it is stored, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have any questions after reading this document, please see the contact information at the end.

1. Information We Collect

ConcreteWiz is designed to minimise data collection. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not transmit any of your inputs or results over the network.

1.1 Information you provide — stored locally on your device

When you use ConcreteWiz, the following data is saved on the device using Apple's UserDefaults framework and never leaves your iPhone or iPad:

CategoryExamplesWhy we store it
Calculation inputs Concrete grade, placement element, mixer model and capacity, target pour volume, sand moisture, stone moisture, sand mud content, custom adjust ratios Persist your work across app launches so you can continue where you left off
Calculation history Material quantities per m³, per batch and total volume, together with the timestamp and the input parameters used to produce the result Let you review, re-apply, or delete previous mix designs from the Workbench
Favorite presets Saved combinations of grade, usage, mixer, moisture and mud content that you have starred with the ⭐️ button Let you re-apply frequently used scenarios with a single tap

1.2 Information collected automatically by the platform

We do not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in ConcreteWiz. However, Apple's iOS operating system itself may collect standard diagnostic information according to the settings you have chosen in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Such data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and is not accessible to the ConcreteWiz developer unless you explicitly opt to share it with app developers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To perform calculations. The input parameters you enter are used exclusively to compute theoretical and on-site concrete mix quantities on the device.
  • To persist state. Local preferences, history records and favorite presets are restored the next time you open ConcreteWiz.
  • To enable share sheets. If you tap the Share button, a plain-text summary of the current mix design is generated and handed to iOS's UIActivityViewController. Which service (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Notes, etc.) receives the text is entirely your choice; ConcreteWiz itself sends nothing.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose other than operating the features listed above.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

  • No sale of personal information. We never sell, rent, or license your data to any third party.
  • No backend service. There is no cloud component, so your records cannot be shared with us or with any partner.
  • You choose when to share. The only way a mix design leaves your device is if you voluntarily tap Share and pick a destination app. Any recipient you choose will handle the shared text under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements. If ConcreteWiz were ever required by law to disclose data, we could only comply to the extent information is in our possession — which, for the avoidance of doubt, is none.

4. Retention and Deletion

All locally stored data remains on your device until you choose to remove it.

  • Delete one history record. Go to the Workbench tab, swipe or tap the delete button next to the record, and confirm.
  • Clear all history. Go to Profile → Data Management → Clear All History Records. This action is irreversible.
  • Delete favorite presets. Favorites can be removed from the Favorites section in the Workbench tab.
  • Delete everything at once. If you uninstall ConcreteWiz from your device, all local records (inputs, history, favorites) are removed together with the app.

5. Security

ConcreteWiz stores its records inside the app's sandboxed container on your device. This container is protected by the iOS file-system encryption tied to your device passcode / Face ID / Touch ID when you have a passcode set. Because no data is transmitted off the device, there is no network transport to intercept and no cloud database to breach.

Note: If you use iTunes / Finder backup or iCloud Backup, ConcreteWiz's UserDefaults data may be included in those backups according to the settings you chose in iTunes / Finder or iCloud. Such backups are covered by the Apple and iCloud privacy policies, not by this one.

6. Children's Privacy

ConcreteWiz is a general-purpose engineering calculator and is not directed at, nor marketed to, children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us; however, because ConcreteWiz does not operate a server, no such submission is technically possible in the first place.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you. For ConcreteWiz, all such data is visible directly inside the app (Workbench history and favorites).
  • Rectify inaccurate data. You can always edit the input parameters on the Calculator tab and re-save the result.
  • Erase your data. Use the per-item delete buttons in Workbench or Clear All History Records in Profile, or uninstall the app.
  • Object or restrict processing. Because we do not perform processing beyond local state persistence, there is no processing pipeline to restrict other than by ceasing to use the app and/or deleting it.

Requests directed to the developer will, where applicable, be addressed within the legal time limits; please note that the developer holds no copies of your data and therefore cannot export or remove data that lives solely on your device.

8. International Transfers

Because ConcreteWiz performs all processing on your device, no personal data is transferred to, stored in, or processed in any other country by the app itself. Any cross-border transfers that do occur would be the result of your own use of the Share button, Apple's iCloud Backup, or similar user-initiated services, which are outside the scope of this policy.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes we will (a) update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and (b) if required by applicable law, notify you through the App Store release notes or via an in-app notice. Your continued use of ConcreteWiz after the effective date of a revised policy constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

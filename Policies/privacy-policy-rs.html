Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

RallyStart ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how RallyStart handles your information.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

RallyStart does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Account credentials or login information
  • Location data
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Health or fitness data
  • Contacts or calendar information
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Browsing history or usage analytics

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All data generated by your use of RallyStart is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults). This includes:

  • Training progress — your best scores for each sport and training phase
  • Session history — records of your completed training sessions, including scores and decisions
  • Achievements — badges you have unlocked and their unlock timestamps
  • Daily challenge progress — today's challenge state and your streak count
  • Onboarding status — whether you have viewed the introductory guide

This data never leaves your device. We have no servers, no cloud storage, and no database infrastructure.

3. No Network Communication

RallyStart is a fully offline application. The app does not make any network requests, does not communicate with any servers, and does not transmit any data over the internet.

4. No Third-Party Services

RallyStart does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, social media SDKs, or external libraries. The app uses only Apple's native frameworks (SwiftUI, Foundation, AVFoundation, UIKit).

5. Permissions Used

RallyStart may request the following system permissions for feature functionality only:

  • Audio — to play system sound effects during training sessions (correct/incorrect answer feedback). No audio is recorded or transmitted.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, or any other sensitive data.

6. Data Deletion

All app data can be permanently deleted at any time:

  • Use the "Reset All Data" option in the app's Settings page to clear all training history, achievements, and progress from your device.
  • Alternatively, deleting the app from your device will remove all associated data.

7. Children's Privacy

RallyStart does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app contains no social features, no user-generated content, and no communication capabilities.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

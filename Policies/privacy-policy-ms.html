MomentSense Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MomentSense mobile application ("the App"). We value your privacy and aim to explain transparently how information is handled.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration or login. We do not actively collect personal information that directly identifies you (such as your name, phone number, email address, or government ID).

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide training and progress tracking, the App stores the following data only on your device:

  • Training progress (completed levels/questions, etc.)
  • Training statistics (score, accuracy, attempts, etc.)
  • Training records (question title, time used, score, correctness, date, etc.)

This data is used for in-app display and is not uploaded to our servers by default.

3. How We Use Information

Local data is used only to:

  • Display your training progress, stats, and history
  • Support continuing training and showing explanations
  • Improve stability and user experience (local functionality only)

4. Sharing & Third-Party Services

  • The App does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • The App does not upload your training data to our servers.

Apple may collect certain information when you download and use the App via the App Store, subject to Apple's policies. We do not control such collection.

5. Permissions

The App typically does not require sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, location, Bluetooth, Health data, or advertising tracking.

If future versions introduce features requiring permissions, we will disclose the purpose via system prompts and update this policy.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your training data is stored on-device.
  • You may delete local data by uninstalling the App (system behavior).

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize data collection and transmission. By default, the App does not transmit training data over the network, reducing risk. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for engineering/structural mechanics learning scenarios and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date will be updated accordingly. Please review periodically.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the developer contact information shown on the App Store listing.

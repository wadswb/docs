Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

StarAlign ("the App") is an educational tool for telescope operation training. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection.

  • No personal information is requested or collected.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs are used.
  • No advertising or marketing technologies are integrated.
  • Training progress and scores are stored locally on your device only and never leave it.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs. No data is shared with any external parties.

Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages and does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted within the App. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.
